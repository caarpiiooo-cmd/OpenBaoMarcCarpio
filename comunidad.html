Community

OpenBao is built by a community under open governance principles. Here is how to join and who supports it.

Get involved

The best way to support the project is to contribute. Read the contribution guide, submit issues and join the discussion.

Contribution guide GitHub repository

Companies, individuals or other entities who actively sponsor the development of OpenBao, with code, non-code contributions or financial support.
