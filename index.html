THE LINUX FOUNDATION PROJECTS

Manage, store, and distribute sensitive data with OpenBao

OpenBao is an open source, community-driven secrets manager and fork of Vault managed by the Linux Foundation’s OpenSSF.

What is OpenBao?Try it out!

Use cases

Secure Secret Storage

Key/value secrets are encrypted before being written to storage.

Dynamic Secrets

Generate secrets on demand and revoke them when the lease is up.

Data Encryption

Encryption as a service with centralized key management.

Identity based access

A unified ACL system brokers access across providers.

Leasing and Renewal

Every secret has a lease and can be renewed via the API.

Revocation

Revoke single secrets or whole trees of secrets.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is OpenBao’s mission statement?

To maintain and improve a software solution to manage, store, and distribute sensitive data including secrets, certificates, and keys, under an OSI-approved open-source license.

What is the status of the fork?

Active, with regular releases.

Which version of HashiCorp Vault are you planning to fork from?

The last version released under the MPL 2.0 license.

How do I get involved?

Visit the Community page.

Will the existing MPL 2.0 licensed code be migrated to another license?

No, it stays under MPL 2.0.
